# Networking

## What is the Networking page for?

It is where you keep track of the *conversations* with the people in your job hunt, where [Contacts](./contacts.md) keeps track of the people themselves. Each conversation is an **interaction**: who it was with, when, why you reached out, what came of it, and what you plan to do next.

Open **Networking** in the sidebar. **People** on the left lists your contacts with where they work, when you last spoke and how many next steps are still open. **Follow-ups due** across the top shows the steps that need you today. **Interactions** below is the timeline, everyone's by default, or one person's when you pick them on the left.

## How do I log an interaction?

Click **Log Interaction**. Pick the **Contact**, the **Purpose** and the **Date**, then add anything worth remembering under **Outcome**. Contact, Purpose and Date are the only required fields.

If the person is not in your contacts yet, use **New Contact** in the People panel first; it opens the same dialog as **Library → Contacts**.

**Related job** is optional. Use it when the conversation was about a specific application, such as a referral request for a job you are tracking. If you later delete that job, the interaction stays and simply loses the link.

To change or remove one, use the pencil and trash icons on its row in the timeline.

## What are purposes, and how do I add my own?

A purpose is the reason you reached out. **Cold Application**, **Advice Call**, **Referral Request**, **Follow-up**, **Thank-you**, **Coffee Chat** and **Intro Request** are there the first time you open the page. They are yours: type a name that does not exist in the Purpose box to create it on the spot, or click **Purposes** to rename or delete them.

A purpose that is in use cannot be deleted. The **Purposes** dialog shows how many interactions use each one; change or delete those interactions first.

## How do next steps and follow-ups work?

Write what you plan to do under **Next step** and, if it has a deadline, give it a **Next step date**. The date cannot be earlier than the interaction itself, and a date needs a step to go with it.

On the day a step is due, it appears under **Follow-ups due**, and it stays there, marked *Overdue since*, until you click **Done**. A step with no date never appears in that list, because it is never due. It stays on its row in the timeline, where you can still mark it done.

**Done** can be undone with **Reopen** on the timeline row. If you edit the step or its date, it counts as a new step and opens again.

## Does logging an interaction update Last contacted?

Yes. **Last contacted** on the contact moves forward to the interaction's date, and only forward: an interaction you log late, for a date before your last contact, does not pull it back. An interaction dated in the future is a plan, so it does not count until its day.

Editing an interaction is treated as removing the old one and logging the new one. Move its date earlier, or change its contact, and the **Last contacted** of both people is worked out again.

Deleting the interaction that set **Last contacted** puts it back to the date of the latest earlier interaction with that person. If there is none, the date is left as it is, since you may have typed it in yourself.

## What happens when I delete a contact?

Their interactions go with them. The confirmation says how many interactions will be deleted. If you only want to remove one conversation, delete that interaction from the timeline instead.

Interactions are part of the backup you can export under **Settings → Data**, and come back with it.
